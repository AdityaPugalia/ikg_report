\section{Repository layout}
\label{app:layout}

\begin{description}\small
\item[\code{models/}] the graph data model (entities, edges, connectors, the graph container), the dimension registry, the sympy bridge, the company store, calibration.
\item[\code{store.py}] SQLite persistence for the graph and the document store.
\item[\code{document\_adapters/}] contract and registry; \code{mineru\_pdf/}, \code{plain\_text/} (incl.\ the EDGAR HTML reader), \code{docfinqa\_markdown/}; the cover reading and the heading outline.
\item[\code{ingestion/}] contract and registry; v1 (facts into the graph), v2 (text and tables, no facts).
\item[\code{retrieval/}] contract and registry; v1 (the solver, linker and text retriever), v2 (the ladder and the table index), v3 (the learned scorer, its features, model, training and calibration).
\item[\code{agent\_loops/}] contract and registry; v2, v3 and v4 (understand, targets, documents, presence, routes, planner, finder, responder, evaluator, trace).
\item[\code{sources/}, \code{bridge/}, \code{secgraph/}] the source graph, the bridge, and the SEC concept-graph build and verifier.
\item[\code{knowledge/}] every knowledge base, one folder each: authored inputs tracked, built files ignored.
\item[\code{scripts/}, \code{viz/}, \code{tests/}, \code{docs/}] drivers for the benchmarks and the builds; the trace viewer and the knowledge explorer; 818 tests; the architecture and data-flow documents.
\end{description}
